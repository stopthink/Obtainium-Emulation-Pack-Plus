## Getting Started

### 1. Install Obtainium

Download the [latest release of Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium/releases/latest) and install it on your device. If you're unsure which APK to grab, use `app-release.apk`.

### 2. Import the Pack

This is the easiest way to get started, especially on a new device.

1. On your device, go to the [latest release](https://github.com/stopthink/Obtainium-Emulation-Pack-Plus/releases/latest).
2. Download the JSON for your device:
   - **Standard** (`obtainium-emulation-pack-vX.X.X.json`) - most devices
   - **Dual-Screen** (`obtainium-emulation-pack-dual-screen-vX.X.X.json`) - dual-screen devices (AYN Thor, Anbernic RG DS, etc.)
3. In Obtainium, go to **Import/Export** > **Obtainium Import** and select the file.

Obtainium will now track and update all included apps.

### What the Plus pack adds

On top of everything in the upstream pack:

- **Utilities:** LocalSend, aShell You, Shizuku, Tailscale, UniPatcher, AdGuard, RootlessJamesDSP, ReVanced Manager, Morphe Manager, a prebuilt Morphe/ReVanced Extended YouTube, WiiUDownloader (Android) and Megingiard (AYN Thor, dual-screen pack only)
- **Native Ports:** Project Picori (Minish Cap), Ship of Harkinian (Ocarina of Time), 2 Ship 2 Harkinian (Majora's Mask), Zelda3 (A Link to the Past) and Dusklight (Twilight Princess). The dual-screen pack swaps in the AYN Thor dual-screen builds of Picori, Zelda3 and Dusklight. All ports need your own legally dumped ROM.
